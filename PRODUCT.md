# Seen

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

Expo, React Native, TypeScript, Expo Router and Supabase, as specified in the supplied development specification. iPhone first; US region and English initially.

## Users

Weekly movie and TV viewers who want to remember what they watched, express their taste and choose their next watch.

## Product Purpose

Remember what you watched. Discover what you'll love. Success is fast logging, a recognizable personal ranking and a useful watch decision.

## Positioning

Short optional comparisons build a personal preference order and automatically assign a Rank Score. No manually entered numeric ratings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four tabs: Home, Discover, Rankings, Watchlist. Profile opens the private journal from the Home header. Local collections, journal search and dated yearly recaps add organization without additional tabs. Liked/Fine/Disliked. Separate movie and whole-show TV rankings; TV requires explicit seen-enough confirmation. Private by default. Comparisons never block logging. Uncompared titles have no score. Backend, auth, social and supplier access are separate release gates.

## Brand Commitments

Seen wordmark in system typography. The three supplied v2 boards govern the initial visual direction. Dark default; large poster artwork, warm ivory controls and quiet provisional labels.

## Evidence on Hand

Full product spec, execution backlog and three v2 ImageGen boards are copied into docs. Existing icon concepts live in output/imagegen. Preview data is illustrative, never actual user activity or provider availability.

## Product Principles

Fast and reversible logging; optional ranking; honest uncertainty; privacy across derived data; native comfort.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Safe areas, system navigation, minimum 44-point targets, text scaling, screen-reader labels and reduced-motion support. No swipe-only actions.
